Specialized Training & Technical Courses
Your situation
"We have engineers working on optical systems, but gaps in the fundamentals are slowing down the project."
Hands-on technical training for engineers, researchers, and R&D teams. Courses are customized to your project context and can be delivered online or on-site.
  • Optical design fundamentals: imaging systems, aberrations, sequential & non-sequential modeling.
  • Advanced optical design: optimization, tolerance analysis, manufacturable specifications.
  • Optical coatings design: from basic multilayers to advanced filter design.
  • Thin-film deposition for optical applications: process, troubleshooting, and QA.
  • Semiconductor devices and optoelectronics: device physics for sensing and energy.

Process Consulting
Thin-Film Deposition Consulting
Your situation
"We have a PVD system but our coatings show batch-to-batch variability. We analyze your system (design + measurements) and identify the root cause of performance issues."
Process support and troubleshooting for PVD thin-film deposition — focused on improving repeatability, uniformity, and optical performance.
  • Process review: materials, monitoring strategy, and deposition planning.

  • Measurement plan recommendations (R/T, thickness, mapping).
